Redline — Symbol Sheet
Tabler Icons (outline, 2 px stroke, round caps — the same grid our custom glyphs use), organized by toolbar tab. Fifteen tools keep a hand-drawn outline on Tabler's grid: the four pens (upright, tip-up close-ups so the nib shape reads at 20 px), shapes (so fill color shows through), line, double arrow and polyline, and bucket fill, text area and radio button, where the pack has no true match so their preset fill or border color can show through the glyph. Each card shows the icon at sheet size, then at the real 20 px toolbar size in its preset color and in the active state.
